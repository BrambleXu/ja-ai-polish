# Voice calibration example

**Repeated traits found in three samples:**

- mostly `です・ます`;
- short first sentence followed by a longer explanation;
- no emoji or rhetorical questions;
- first person used only for an explicit decision;
- parentheses used for versions and measurements.

**Compact voice card:**

> Use polite Japanese. Open sections with a short factual sentence. Keep explanations compact.
> Use `私は` only for the author's supplied choice. Preserve parenthetical technical details.

Do not copy the samples' product names, events, anecdotes, or characteristic phrases into the new
text.
