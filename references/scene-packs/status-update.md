# Status update

- **Goal:** communicate progress, decisions, blockers, risk, owners, and next steps.
- **Audience:** project teams, stakeholders, incident responders, and successors.
- **Register:** factual and direct; match the seriousness of the update.
- **Information order:** completed → in progress → blocker/risk → next step, owner, deadline.
- **Structure:** short labeled sections or bullets when status categories are useful.
- **Personalization:** minimal; permit the author's normal concise voice.
- **Protect:** completion state, incident severity, uncertainty, owners, deadlines, metrics, and
  decisions.
- **Line integrity:** keep each ordinary sentence on one physical line; retain separate status labels
  and action items when they represent real categories, owners, or deadlines. Complete any sentence
  before a list.

## Common failures and exceptions

Remove false optimism, repeated summaries, vague responsibility, and conclusions without action.
Meeting follow-up must retain decisions and action items. Incident text must not weaken risk or turn
an unconfirmed cause into fact.

## Example

**Weak:** `順調に進捗しており、今後も適切に対応していきます。`

**Better when grounded:** `API修正は完了しました。負荷試験は未実施です。佐藤さんが木曜までに確認します。`

**Accept:** `順調です` only when concrete completed work follows.

## Acceptance

Each status and uncertainty level matches the input, and every stated next step has its known owner
and deadline.
