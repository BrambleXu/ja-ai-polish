# Chat message

- **Goal:** enable quick coordination without losing the context needed to act.
- **Audience:** coworkers and project collaborators in Slack, Teams, Chatwork, or similar channels.
- **Register:** concise conversational business Japanese; match the channel and relationship.
- **Information order:** context → current state or conclusion → requested action → reply need/time.
- **Structure:** one compact paragraph or short bullets when multiple actions exist.
- **Personalization:** low to moderate; preserve the author's emoji and punctuation habits.
- **Protect:** owners, deadlines, blockers, links, channel references, and decision status.
- **Line integrity:** keep a sentence on one physical line; use separate lines only for independent
  actions, owners, deadlines, or user-requested message formatting.

## Common failures and exceptions

Remove email-style openings, long preambles, mechanical numbering, and generic praise. Add no emoji,
exclamation mark, or intimacy that is absent from the input. Keep bullets when they separate real
owners or actions.

## Example

**Weak:** `お疲れ様です。まず結論からお伝えします。現在、対応を行っている状況です。`

**Better:** `対応中です。ログ確認は田中さん、修正は私が担当します。15時に状況を共有します。`

**Accept:** a numbered list for three independent handoff items.

## Acceptance

The message states what happened, what is needed, who acts, and when, at a length suitable for chat.
