# Social post

- **Goal:** share one concrete update or grounded opinion clearly.
- **Audience:** followers, peers, and the general public on X or similar services.
- **Register:** concise and natural; preserve the author's degree of formality.
- **Information order:** concrete point → necessary context → grounded view or next step.
- **Structure:** one post or a thread only when requested; respect an explicit character limit.
- **Personalization:** follow supplied voice; never manufacture emotion, surprise, confession, or
  controversy.
- **Protect:** links, tags, handles, dates, release names, numbers, and distinct claims.

## Common failures and exceptions

Remove template hooks, engagement bait, unsupported success/crisis narratives, and hashtag or emoji
stacks. Do not transform a normal update into marketing. Keep a hook when it states a real finding
and fits the author's samples.

## Example

**Weak:** `衝撃の結果です！生成AIが未来を変える3つの理由を解説します。`

**Better when grounded:** `評価データを200件に増やしたところ、検索精度は0.61から0.69に上がりました。設定と結果をまとめます。`

**Accept:** a required campaign hashtag supplied by the user.

## Acceptance

Every claim is grounded, the post fits the requested length, and compression has not silently
removed an independent fact.
