# Work email

- **Goal:** make the purpose, request, deadline, owner, and next step easy to act on.
- **Audience:** coworkers, managers, vendors, customers, and other external business contacts.
- **Register:** choose honorific level from the relationship; keep formal external mail formal and
  internal mail practical.
- **Usage fit:** prefer conventional business-Japanese collocations for the relationship. Keep
  external honorific formulas when they perform relationship work; do not carry chat shorthand into
  formal mail or external-mail formulas into a practical internal note.
- **Information order:** purpose → necessary context → requested action → deadline/next step.
- **Structure:** subject when requested, brief greeting, body, concrete close.
- **Personalization:** restrained; use only relationship-appropriate warmth already requested.
- **Protect:** names, company names, dates, commitments, owners, prices, attachments, and apology
  scope.
- **Line integrity:** keep each ordinary paragraph on one physical line; preserve real breaks between
  greeting, body, close, and signature. Repair a sentence that depends on a list before or after it.

## Common failures and exceptions

Compress repeated greetings, thanks, apologies, buffers, and stacked `させていただく`. Keep
conventional greetings when relationship maintenance matters. Do not turn an internal note into an
official notice or a customer reply into casual chat.

## Example

**Weak:** `ご提案は非常に興味深い内容です。社内で検討を行った上で、改めてご連絡させていただきます。`

**Better when grounded:** `ご提案ありがとうございます。来週火曜の会議で確認し、水曜までに回答します。`

**Accept:** `お世話になっております。` in an established external relationship.

## Acceptance

The recipient can identify the purpose, their action, owner, and deadline without inferring them.
Politeness remains appropriate and no commitment changes.
